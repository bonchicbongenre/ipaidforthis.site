What ipaidforthis.site does. A golf pass must keep every line true.

 1. With script, the link under its label turns through the studio's sites in the
    wall's order, this one among them, slowing evenly to a stop as a wheel does,
    and stops on one chosen at random, each as likely as the next; every one passes
    at least once before it stops. One time in four it seems to stop one short, and
    then goes over. It rests on the one it stops on for 0.9 seconds and takes the
    visitor there, 2.5 to 4.8 seconds in all. If it stops on this site, it takes
    the visitor here: the page loads again, and turns again. It replaces itself in
    the history, so the way back does not come through here. With reduced motion it does not turn: it shows the one chosen, and goes. A
    screen reader hears the name it stops on.
 2. Every path on it does the same: the 404 is the same page, byte for byte.
 3. Without script, it stays: its label, with the price as its dimensions, and one
    plain link, to the wall.
 4. Its list of sites is held to the wall by the wall's own check: a site on the
    wall and not on the list, or on the list and not on the wall, fails it.
 5. The title (the tab, a link's preview) is "I paid for this."; a preview adds
    "It was on sale."
 6. Colours come from the house tokens, Ru. The theme button, as on every site,
    shows with script, for as long as the visitor is here.
 7. The favicon is two pips on a rounded square, in Ru.
